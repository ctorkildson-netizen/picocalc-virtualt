PROGRAMS FOR VIRTUALT
=====================
(Best read in a fixed-width font, such as Consolas or Courier
New in Notepad: Format > Font.)

VirtualT comes with 55 Model 100 and Tandy 200 programs,
all run and checked on a PicoCalc. They are in the m100 and m200
folders of the download: copy those folders into virtualt/ on the
SD card. To load one, start VirtualT, press Ctrl+F4, pick it and
press Enter (VIRTUALT.TXT, QUICK START, has the whole walk-through).
Load a program's .DO or .DOC file too if it has one.

Almost all of them come from the CompuServe M100SIG library of
the 1980s, kept today at web8201.net and linked from Club 100
(club100.org), which has thousands more. The authors' names and
comments are left as they wrote them. Where we fixed something,
a line starting "2026:" near the top says what; everything else
is unchanged. If you wrote one of these and would rather it
weren't included, open an issue on the project page and it will
be removed.

Many of these programs expect capital letters. If one ignores
your Y or N, press Caps Lock. Where a program says F6, F7 or F8,
press Shift+F1, F2 or F3 on the PicoCalc.

MODEL 100 (m100)
----------------
GAMES

BLKJAK.BA    Tim Lockwood, 1985. A Las Vegas quarter machine.
 + BLKJAK.DOC Bet, then h hit, s stand, d double.

CHESS.BA     Mike Weiblen, 1985, with an opening book added in
             2026, so its first moves are instant. Moves as from-to
             squares (E2E4) and Enter; K and Q castle, P passes.

CRIBGE.BA    James McGreevy, 1986. Cribbage to 121, scored
 + CRIBGE.DOC correctly. Number keys pick cards, G is Go. Count
             your own hands: miss points and it calls MUGGINS.
             Its discards take about 30 seconds.

DRAGON.BA    Barry Robinson, 1985. Gold, a Ring, Smaug and the
             Arkenstone over three cave levels. Arrows move, U/D
             change level. The Balrog kills without warning.

ELIZA.BA     Jack Shalom, 1985. The 1966 psychotherapist: type a
             sentence, it turns it around.

GREED.BA     Joe Wasserman, 1987. Six dice: 1s, 5s and triples
 + GREED.DOC score; bank or roll again, and a scoreless roll
             loses the turn. Up takes a triple, Left a 1, Right a
             5, Down rolls, Enter banks.

HIQ.BA       Michael Martell, 1985. Peg solitaire: type a peg
             (D2), then an arrow for the jump.

INVADR.BA    James Yi, 1986. A row of aliens sweeps and descends.
             , and . move, Space fires; 4 waves, 5 lives. Menu
             keys are lowercase. Shots cost points.

LANDER.BA    Randy Cassingham, 1983. A small graphic lander;
 + LANDER.DOC thrust 0-9 stays set until you change it.

LEARN.BA     Frank Kienast, 1986. Twenty Questions that learns:
 + ANIMAL.DO wrong guesses teach it a new question. Load both, press
             1 and type ANIMAL, 3 plays, 5 saves before 6 quits.
             Caps Lock on, no commas in answers.

LIFE.BA      Written for VirtualT. Arrows move, Space flips a
             cell, R random, C clear, Enter runs, any key stops,
             Q quits. Says when the colony dies or freezes.

LUNAR.BA     Jim Storer, 1969, BASIC by David Ahl: the original
             lunar lander. Every 10 seconds, a burn rate.

MAZE3D.BA    Tim Ekdom, 1984. Escape a 5x5x5 cube of rooms drawn
             in perspective. N S E W U D move, F faces, * and ?
             show where you are and the exit. Quit with F8.

MICROI.BA    Mike Berro, 1983. "Micro-Invaders": one invader, its
 + MICROI.DOC bombs falling in your column. , . move (< > by 5),
             Space fires, Esc fires a homing laser. Capital Y/N.

MURDER.BA    Benn Gibson, 1984. Name who, with what and where by
             first letters (Enter lists them). Each wrong guess
             rules one out, and the alibi clue clears two more if
             you read it right. Any key skips the opening tune.

OTHELO.BA    "jt", Don Zeikel, James Shelton, 1984-87. Caps Lock
             off; moves are row then column (3d). About 20
             seconds a move early on.

POKER.BA     Karl Chase, 1984. Five-card draw, jacks to open,
 + POKER.DOC against five opponents who bluff and false-draw. C
             calls, F folds, a number raises; discards by
             position (125), Enter stands pat.

PONG.BA      Rick Perry, 1983. One paddle, a dropping ceiling.
             Esc 1 Space = Backspace jump the paddle; 2 - nudge.

QZ-100.BA    Bill Templeton, 1987. True/false quizzes, 24 questions
 + ARTS.DO   each, against the clock: Disney, arts, boxing, horse
 + BOXING.DO racing. Load the quiz files with it. The facts are
 + DISNEY.DO 1987's, which is half the fun.
 + HORSES.DO

ROG.BA       Willard Goosey, 1998-2016 (GPL). Random dungeons,
             monsters a-z, Body/Mind/Soul stats that double as
             spell points. h j k l (or arrows), y u b n diagonals,
             > < stairs; z Mind attack, p prayer, a heal, s saves.

SHARKS.BA    Ray Yeargin, 1985. Wa-Tor: fish and sharks breed,
 + SHARKS.DOC hunt and starve. Try 40 8 80 10 3 3 4 3 1 1 at the
             questions; lowercase t shows the tally.

SOL31C.BA    Philip Avery, 2015. Klondike with real card faces,
 + SOLCR3.CO drawn by a machine-code routine: load both. I and O
             move, P picks up, D drops, N next card, F foundation.

STARF.BA     Mike Berro, 1983. Enemies fly in from the right; your
 + STARFI.DOC laser hits the nearest in your row. Arrows move,
             Space or - fires, P pauses. Capital Y/N. Load
             STARFI.DOC for its instructions.

TOOLS

ALARMS.BA    Rick Perry, 1984. A background alarm that beeps
             wherever you are (F1 time, F2 on). Don't use it with
             machine-code programs such as SOLCR3.CO.

CARTPT.BA    J. Holovacs, 1987. Plots x,y data from the keyboard
 + CARTPT.DOC or a .DO file (a two-column CSV works); linear or
 + GROW.DO   log scales. F2 replots, F3 exits.

CHIMES.BA    Paul Carnell, 1983. A Regulator wall clock with
             Westminster chimes; keys 1-4 chime now. F8 menu.

DBNEW.BA     Bob Ripley, 1984. A flat-file database in a .DO file:
 + DBNEW.DOC sort, search, totals, labels. Try it on DBTEST.DO.
 + DBTEST.DO

FACTRL.BA    A 1987 contest one-liner: 1!, 2!, 3! ... exactly.

FUNPLT.BA    James Scales, 1988. Plots X=F(Z), Y=F(Z): graphs and
             curves. Keep the numbers modest.

HP35.BA      Larry Reddig, 1987. The original HP-35, RPN, x only.

HPCALC.BA    Scott Schad, 1987 (shareware). An HP-41-style RPN
 + HPCALC.DOC calculator; Up/Down pick function rows.

KEPLER.BA    Don Sakers, 1988. Builds habitable planets and star
 + KEPLER.DOC systems for science fiction writers. Caps Lock off.

LOGIC.BA     J. Holovacs, 1986. Logic-circuit simulator with
 + LOGIC.DOC AND/OR/XOR/NAND/NOR gates. T, F, U in capitals.

RPNCAL.BA    Terry Morgan, 1984. A full four-level RPN calculator
 + RPNCAL.DOC with stats and registers. Caps Lock on.

SNOOZR.BA    Bob Luley, 1984. Alarm clock playing the 1812
             Overture; Space snoozes.

SPREAD.BA    Woods Martin, 1986. A real little spreadsheet, 20 x 19
 + SPRTST.DO cells, with @SUM @AVG @MIN @MAX and more. Wants about
             24K free, so remove other programs first.

SYLCAT.BA    Paul Globman, 1990. Checks syllogisms: "all men are
             mortal" style premises, then a conclusion.

TYPING.BA    St. Petersburg Police Department, 1984. A typing
 + TYPING.DOC tutor that names the finger for every key; types
 + TEST.DO   from TEST.DO. Record to beat: 13 wpm.

VIBES.BA     Machine Design, 1986. Masses on springs, Runge-Kutta.

WMAP.BA      An interactive world map: arrows move, L shows the
             place and its local time, S/D measure distance. Set
             XZ=-5 in line 18 to your time zone (-8 Pacific).

WORLD.BA     Henry Glover, 1989. A world map; 1 adds time zones.



TANDY 200 (m200)
----------------

CHIME.BA     A bell that fades away. Run MKCHIM once to build
 + MKCHIM.BA CHIME.CO, then CHIME asks for pitch and duration.

CLOCK2.BA    An analog clock, white for a.m., black for p.m.
             Caps Lock off: c ticks, a alarm, s set, t time.

CLONES.BA    Fred Brown. Grab the $ before the clones catch you;
             Space makes you safe for three moves.

FLYING.BA    Graphics demo.

FRACTL.BA    Fractals, slowly.

LIFE.BA      As on the Model 100, on a 40x15 field; G loads
             Gosper's glider gun.

OTHELL.BA    Othello. Answers in capitals.

QUEST1.BA    Fred Brown, 1988. A dungeon quest: tavern, 101 rooms,
             a dragon and a crown. Buy armor and a weapon first,
             F5 saves. Caps Lock on.

SCROLL.BA    James Yi, 1986. Scrolls the screen a pixel row at a
             time by reprogramming the LCD. Any key ends it.

SHT200.BA    Curtis Karnow. Land the Space Shuttle. Arrows set
             angle and turn, G gear, S/C spoilers. Keep the gear up
             and dive 5-8 degrees; flatten only at the end.
             Shift+Esc stops it afterwards.

SLOTS.BA     Leo Finkelstein Jr. A slot machine with graphics and
             a running win/loss chart. Number keys bet.

SOLIT.BA     Klondike in graphics characters. Arrows move, N
             deals, P picks up, D drops, F foundation.

SUBWAR.BA    Fred Brown, 1987. Submarine against destroyer, real
             time, with periscope, sonar and depth charges. Caps
             Lock on; Y at the start for instructions.



ALSO WORTH A LOOK (not included)
--------------------------------
These work too, but their authors asked that they not be passed
on, so get them from the M100SIG library yourself:

CLOCK.PMS    Peter Stanwyck, 1986. A watch face with sweeping
             hands, alarm, timer and stopwatch. Rename to
             CLOCK.BA. M returns to the menu.

NAV2.BAS     Gary Van Balen, 1991. A highway trip log with ETAs
 + NAV2.DOC  for each milestone; make the map file in TEXT first.
